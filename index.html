VIRTUAL REALITY · ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE · HEALTHCARE

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University (KSU) and a Graduate Research Assistant in the XRei Lab (eXtended Reality and Intelligence).

My research brings together Virtual Reality and Artificial Intelligence for healthcare. I'm interested in making healthcare interventions and rehabilitation more adaptive, personalized, and effective. I explore how Reinforcement Learning and Large Language Models can help immersive environments adapt to individuals' needs, behaviors, and changing conditions.

I completed my B.Sc. in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh. Before joining KSU, I taught Computer Science and Engineering at BUFT and Bangladesh University.

Projects

CURRENT PROJECTVR & AIIn progress

Cognitive Overload Detection and Forecasting in VR

Developing a multimodal approach to detecting current cognitive overload and forecasting overload within the next 30 seconds during VR tasks. The work brings together gaze, movement, physiological signals, and task behavior with time-resolved annotations.

The broader project explores how everyday-task environments, such as virtual grocery shopping, could support personalized cognitive assistance.

Virtual Reality · Multimodal Sensing · Cognitive Overload · Temporal Forecasting

UNDERGRADUATE PROJECTNLP

Intelligent Information Retrieval via Question Answering

Knowledge Graphs · Graph Neural Networks · Semantic Reasoning
